# Shadows

Shadow tokens come from the Figma effect styles Shadow/Card and Shadow/Lift (`tokens/effects.json`) and
are built into `shared.css` as `--shadow-card` and `--shadow-lift`. Their colours are the Semantic
tokens `--color-shadow-card` and `--color-shadow-lift`, which carry the alpha (8-digit hex) and change
with the theme. The Dark values are proposals.
