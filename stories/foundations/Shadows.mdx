# Shadows

Shadow tokens come from the Figma effect styles Shadow/Card and Shadow/Lift (`tokens/effects.json`) and
are built into `shared.css` as `--shadow-card` and `--shadow-lift`. A shadow bound to a colour variable
keeps it as a reference, so `--shadow-card` follows `--color-shadow` in every brand and theme.
